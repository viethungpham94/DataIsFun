Foreword

Hey guys,

Having lived and worked across different corners of the world, I've noticed a recurring pattern in how we talk: we speak in hypotheses, but argue as if they're facts. We love reaching for data, but we aren't always well-equipped to handle data impartially.

Take sports: fans passionately quote expected goals, completion rates, or trophies won, carefully selecting metrics that validate their favourite player while discounting counter-evidence. When the stakes rise to social issues or policy debates, that blind spot can cause real damage.

This blog is an effort to cut through that noise. I want to unpack polarising questions and fascinating debates using transparent data, stripped of academic gatekeeping and heavy math so anyone interested in the topic can dig in.

Please consider this a collaborative sandbox. I can't claim to be an expert, I'm just a data enthusiast genuinely eager to see where the evidence leads. I'd love to hear your feedback or topics you would like us to explore together.

Across 1,915 senior goals, 774 assists, and 25 shared seasons across club and international football:

  • Messi leads in all-round playmaking (+160 assists, 2.3× Big Chances Created), non-penalty scoring (+24 NPG), finishing efficiency (+37.95 Opta 13-season league xG surplus margin), total attacking volume (1,404 vs 1,286 G+A), ball progression (3,093 vs 1,655 dribbles), goals vs FIFA Top-20 nations (34 vs 29), and FIFA World Cup knockout delivery (7 vs 1 knockout goals; 21 vs 11 overall).
  • Ronaldo leads in gross career volume (+43 gross goals: 979 vs 936), penalty execution (+67 converted PKs: 186 vs 119), aerial duel dominance (+616 Opta aerial duels won; +103 headers), UEFA Champions League scoring crowns (+1) & titles (5 vs 4), and carried squads with lower average on-pitch teammate market value (€33.2M vs €37.5M).
  • Knockout game-winners stand at an exact dead heat: 44–44 career game-winning goals in knockout matches (while Messi leads total knockout goals 152 vs 133).

Approach

Applying four core principles to ensure validity, readability, and transparency

01 / TESTABLE
Broken-Down Assumptions
We believe any opinion can be broken into comprehensive sets of testable statements. This process lets us blueprint narratives into clear dimensions where variables can be isolated and evaluated.
02 / STORYLINED
Evidence-Backed Narratives
People think in stories, statements, and claims. We present our evidence and findings in a clear narrative sequence, making deep analysis accessible to everyone.
03 / VISUALISED
Easy-to-Read Graphics
The most effective presentation is the simplest one. We avoid academic jargon and heavy formulas, presenting each insight through approachable charts.
04 / TRACKABLE
Clear Audit Trail
Even data-driven work involves analytical choices. A complete audit trail accompanies every release, linking directly to raw data sources and methodological rules.
