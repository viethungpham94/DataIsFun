Appendix 2 Part III · Metric Definitions & Calibration

3. Standardized Definitions & Calibration

  • Gross vs Official Competitive Goals (D-001, D-009): Gross senior goals (N=1,915: 936 Messi, 979 Ronaldo) include senior international friendlies (55 Messi, 22 Ronaldo). Official competitive goals (N=1,838: 881 Messi, 957 Ronaldo) exclude all club/international friendlies and penalty shootouts.
  • Season Calendar Mapping (D-002): European & Saudi clubs follow Aug–Jul seasons (2002-03 to 2026-27*); MLS calendar-year seasons (2023–2026) and summer international tournaments map to the corresponding unified campaign.
  • Biological Age & Breakthrough Year: Exact age on match date is (match_date - dob) / 365.25. Breakthrough Year 1 is defined as first top-flight senior season (2002-03 Sporting CP for Ronaldo; 2004-05 FC Barcelona for Messi).
  • Zero-Estimation Multi-Source Distance & Expected Goals (xG) Policy (enrich_goals_xg_distance.py):
    1) Tier 1 (Understat Shot-Level Archive): Validated 2014/15–2018/19 [X, Y] shot coordinates (N=331 goals: 176 Messi, 155 Ronaldo).
    2) Tier 2 (FBref / Opta Shooting Logs via worldfootballR_data): Domestic league 2017/18–2024 xG and yardage (converted via × 0.9144 m/yd, empirical ratio vs FotMob 0.9125) for N=178 additional goals (99 Messi, 79 Ronaldo).
    3) Tier 3 (FotMob Shot Details via worldfootballR_data): 2020/21–2024 UCL, UEFA Euro, UEL, MLS & league 105m × 68m coordinates (excluding shootouts & own goals) for N=41 additional goals (14 Messi, 27 Ronaldo).
    4) Tier 4 (StatsBomb Open Data): Historical La Liga (Messi 2004–2021), UCL Finals, World Cup & Euro events for N=264 additional goals (251 Messi, 13 Ronaldo).
    5) Tier 5 & Uninstrumented Policy: External penalties (N=167) receive IFAB Law 14 spot geometry (11.0m, 0.7564 xG); remaining N=934 goals stay NaN. Seasonal Avg xG/Goal and Avg Distance curves include only is_telemetry == True goals in seasons with ≥5 telemetry goals and ≥3 non-penalty telemetry goals so uninstrumented eras never collapse to flat 0.756 xG / 11.0m lines.
  • Game Winning Goals (is_gwg): A goal that broke a tie score (team_at_score == opp_at_score + 1) in a match won by the player's team (team_final_score > opp_final_score), verified across 100% (1,915/1,915) of goals.
Appendix 2 Part IV · Multi-Source Fusion & Coverage Boundaries

4. Explicit Coverage Gaps & Multi-Source Fusion Policy

  • Multi-Source Telemetry Fusion (Overcoming StatsBomb's Asymmetry): Relying on StatsBomb Open Data alone creates severe bias (648 Messi vs 20 Ronaldo matches), while relying on Understat alone restricts telemetry to 2014–2019. Therefore, we combine all 4 shot-telemetry providers in strict fixture priority (Understat > FBref/Opta > FotMob > StatsBomb) to lift shot-level goal coverage from 327 to 814 goals (42.5%) and assemble 4,609 deduplicated shots (combined_shots.csv, including 3,792 missed shots) across 2004–2025, paired with explicit seasonal coverage charts (1.1.3) and Spatial pill indicators (Extra 3).
  • Understat Mirror Verification & 2019–2020 Discrepancy Exclusion: Because direct scraping of understat.com returns an error stub, raw mirrors in data/raw/understat_mirror/2026-11-11/ are SHA-256 hashed in MANIFEST.md and cross-reconciled season-by-season in data/validation/understat_reconciliation.csv. Seasons 2015/16–2018/19 reconcile to 0.0 shot / 0.000 xG variance; Ronaldo's 2019-2020 slice in understat_all_shots.csv is truncated (10 vs 208 shots), flagged DISCREPANCY, and automatically excluded from all downstream xG, conversion, and spatial charts.
  • Goals per 90 Minutes (G/90) Window: Match-level minutes played in messi_performance_game.json and ronaldo_performance_game.json from Transfermarkt Direct API cover the entire 2002–2027 career span (N=3,260 match records) across every season with ≥400 minutes.
  • Body-Part & Direct Free Kick Audit: Of 1,915 career goals, 1,682 have explicit body-part tags (855 Messi, 827 Ronaldo) and 233 remain unspecified. Verified senior direct free kicks total 65 (Messi) vs 55 (Ronaldo), excluding youth/reserve/friendly claims.
  • Excluded Metrics & Methodological Parity Policy (Removal of 1.3.2 Carry Progression / Multiple Take-On Efficiency & Matchday Rank):
    1) Dribble Effectiveness & Carry Progression (1.3.2 / q4_1_eff): Removed because underlying FBref/Opta match logs omitted Serie A take-on event tracking throughout 2018/19–2020/21 (covering Ronaldo's entire Juventus tenure) and lack pre-2017 wing-era data, while StatsBomb Open Data only covers a single club season (2015/16) for Ronaldo alongside extensive Barcelona history. Keeping non-comparable seasonal carry progression and multi-take-on averages across mismatched windows would violate cross-player methodological parity; Criteria 1.3 now relies strictly on balanced Opta European dribble totals & per-90 rates (1.3.1).
    2) League Goals by Matchday Rank vs Eventual Top-4: Live matchday table rank is noisy early in a season before table positions settle; analysis was consolidated onto eventual end-of-season Top-4 standings (noting the analytical caveat that full-season Top-4 standing does not capture short-term opponent form on matchday).
    3) Unstandardized Commercial & Proprietary Aggregates: Multi-season per-game live TV viewership, confidential Adidas/Nike jersey unit sales, and league-wide 2009–2026 seasonal WhoScored match-rating percentiles are omitted under our zero-estimation policy.
